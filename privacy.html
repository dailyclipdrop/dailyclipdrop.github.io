Privacy Policy — DCD Clips

Last updated: November 21, 2026

DCD Clips ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Daily Clip Drop (@dcd_media). It uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth access token for the owner's own YouTube channel with these permissions: uploading videos, managing the channel's playlists, and reading the channel's basic information (channel ID, title and handle) and the status and view counts of its own videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's Shorts to the owner's channel, add them to the channel's playlists, confirm that the authorised account is the intended channel, and confirm that an upload is public. The app does not access any other user's data and does not use data for advertising.

Storage

The OAuth token and channel ID are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database outside that computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties. A published video's public link is submitted by the owner to clipping campaigns the owner has joined, which is the purpose of publishing it.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access, or deleting the token file from the owner's computer, removes all stored authorisation data. To ask for deletion of any data, contact the address below; requests are handled within 7 days.

Cookies

This website and the app do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
